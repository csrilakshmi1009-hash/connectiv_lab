Display

Video Walls, Active LED & Projectors

A video wall is a multi-screen setup of monitors or TVs tiled together contiguously to form one unified large screen.

Active LED displays are superior to LED display panels in output quality and longevity — higher brightness, better colour reproduction and a bezel-free seamless image. They also allow chip-level repairs.

Display

Commercial LFDs, Signage & Digital Standees

Commercial LFDs bring high-resolution images to indoor applications where image quality matters — from museums and visitor attractions to broadcasting, control rooms, retail and advertising.

Digital standees are floor-standing digital signage. Signage can be wall-mounted or ceiling-hung, but when it has to stand in a corridor or on a floor, a digital standee is used.
