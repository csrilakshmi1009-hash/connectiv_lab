About Us

Connectiv Technologies and Solutions is a new-generation system integrator based in Bengaluru, specializing in sales and integration of AV/IT industry products, and in end-to-end audio-video solutions and integration.

  • We focus on creating new market opportunities by embracing emerging technologies and offering value-added services.
  • We provide solutions and services to Education, Government, Corporate, Retail, Residential, Hospitality and Healthcare sectors.
  • We specialize in commercial and residential audio systems, audio-video conferencing, AV integration, digital signage and home theatre.
  • We work with well-known brands: Samsung, ViewSonic and Panasonic in display solutions; Logitech, HP Poly and Neat in video conferencing; Barco in wireless presentation and conferencing; and QSC and JBL in audio solutions.
  • We are committed to providing the best AV/IT products, services and real value to our customers, building lasting relationships through quality work and dependable support.

Video Walls, Active LED & Projectors

A video wall is a multi-screen setup of monitors or TVs tiled together contiguously to form one unified large screen.

Active LED displays are superior to LED display panels in output quality and longevity — higher brightness, better colour reproduction and a bezel-free seamless image. They also allow chip-level repairs.

Display

Commercial LFDs, Signage & Digital Standees

Commercial LFDs bring high-resolution images to indoor applications where image quality matters — from museums and visitor attractions to broadcasting, control rooms, retail and advertising.

Digital standees are floor-standing digital signage, used when signage has to stand in a corridor or on a floor rather than be wall-mounted or ceiling-hung.
